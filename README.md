# Idea Deck

A personal workspace for capturing, prioritising, and tracking ideas — from first thought to shipped.

---

## What it does

Idea Deck gives you three ways to work with your ideas:

- **Board** — drag ideas across Backlog, In Research, In Progress, and Shipped columns
- **Matrix** — plot every idea by impact vs. effort to spot quick wins at a glance
- **Table** — sort, search, and filter your full idea list in one place

Each idea supports a title, status, impact/effort scores, tags, and a full Markdown description. Deleted ideas move to Trash and are permanently removed after 90 days.
